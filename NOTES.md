## Next

- **Earliest open night** — when the chosen dates are full or not released, show the next night that looks open.
- **Fire risk** — show current fire / restriction info for a campground (e.g. CAL FIRE), not just a generic link.
- **Prompt → shortlist** — user describes the trip in a sentence; the app fills filters and returns a wishlist to review.
- **Watch openings (later)** — for saved wishlist places, notice when nights free and notify. Auto-booking or grabbing cancellations is a separate decision (accounts, booking-site rules).

When an item is in progress, move it to a **Now** section. When it ships, delete it.
