# Iteration notes

Scratch pad for the camping finder. **Not** the public README — visitors should not need this.

Live app: https://ca-family-camping-finder-jtjiang.streamlit.app/  
GitHub: https://github.com/jitingjiang/ca-family-camping-finder

## Next

- **Earliest open night** — when the chosen dates are full or not released, show the next night that looks open.
- **Fire risk** — show current fire / restriction info for a campground (e.g. CAL FIRE), not just a generic link.
- **Weather on the card** — one line with elevation and expected lows for the chosen dates, e.g. `6,200 ft · lows near 34°F on your dates`.
- **One photo per federal campground** — Recreation.gov already has the pictures; this is mostly wiring one into ingest and the card.
- **Beginner badge** — mark places that look like a good first trip (easy access, toilets, not too remote).
- **Prompt → shortlist** — user describes the trip in a sentence; the app fills filters and returns a wishlist to review.
- **Watch openings (later)** — for saved wishlist places, notice when nights free and notify. Auto-booking or grabbing cancellations is a separate decision (accounts, booking-site rules).
- **Day-of packing** — a short “what to bring” list for a first family night (not a full gear encyclopedia).

When an item is in progress, move it to a **Now** section. When it ships, delete it.
